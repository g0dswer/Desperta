# Blind visual fidelity review — retro and nineties

Compared only these four rendered images, with the candidate scaled to the reference content width. OS status/navigation bars and the actual clock/countdown values were ignored. The two identities are **not identical**.

## Retro

### Highest-priority corrections

1. **Rebuild the next-alarm card's missing ornament and timer treatment.** The reference card is approximately 96% of canvas width and about 52% of that width tall; the candidate width is close, but its card is roughly 7–8% shorter after normalization. The reference has a small orange uppercase title, thin orange rules on both sides ending in dark screw heads, and a horizontal divider with a centered dark/metal screw below the countdown. The candidate has only a centered title and no rules, screw heads, or divider. Restore those elements at the same relative positions: title around 5–7% of card height from the top; divider around 75% of card height; bottom summary around 88–92%.
2. **Replace the candidate countdown font and proportions.** The reference timer occupies about 83–85% of card width and uses heavy rounded/geometric navy digits with two round orange colon dots. The candidate timer is only about 68–70% of card width, is shorter, and uses a slashed zero plus square colon marks. Match the reference digit width/height and remove the diagonal slash; use circular orange dots with the reference spacing.
3. **Restore the full alarm-card artwork and layout.** Both candidate alarm-card images are rectangular cropped slices at the far left; the reference has complete circular navy plaques with the briefcase/planet illustrations, metallic rims, and small perforation dots underneath. The candidate text begins around 19% of canvas width, while the reference begins around 29–30%, because the circular icon column is missing. Restore the full masked circles and move time/title/detail right to the reference column.
4. **Fix the lower-page composition.** Candidate alarm cards are around 6–8% taller after normalization and the gap between them is roughly twice the reference gap. The model row is about 20–25% too tall, its bottom is overlapped/occluded by the plus bar, and the plus bar starts too early relative to that row. Match the compact reference sequence: two compact cards, a short model row, a small gap, then the full-height plus bar.
5. **Recreate the reference plus button.** The reference is a deep orange, textured, layered/beveled bar with nested rounded outlines, a circular cream metal plus control in a dark rim at the left, and five narrow vertical ventilation grooves at the right. The candidate is a flatter orange bar: its plus control is a cropped rectangular image block, the label is centered too far right, and the right grooves are absent. Restore the left control around 8–22% of canvas width, label beginning around 28–30%, and grooves in the rightmost 4–8%.

### Other concrete differences

- The candidate global cream is visibly lighter and less warm/aged than the reference, and the candidate cards are close to flat ivory. The reference has a warmer cream, paper-like grain, stains, and fine tonal variation throughout. Preserve the reference orange/navy palette and texture instead of the candidate's cleaner pale fill.
- The reference screen/content silhouette has rounded outer corners with black corner cutouts; the candidate viewport is square at the visible edges and has a bright vertical edge on the right. Keep the app-owned rounded silhouette when it is part of the rendered identity.
- The hero artwork is the closest section, but the candidate hero is a little shorter relative to width, has less visible outer/bezel linework, and leaves roughly twice the normalized gap before the next-alarm card. Tighten the hero-to-card gap and retain the thin rounded frame.
- The candidate logo/gear placement is broadly close, but the candidate loses some of the reference's fine printed/beveled texture and thin frame detail. Keep the logo at about 7% left inset and the gear at the same upper-right inset while restoring the edge treatment.
- Reference list cards have four detailed metal screw heads, layered cream borders, a dark lower/right depth edge, and a colored vertical side strip (orange on Trabalho, navy on Fim de semana). Candidate cards show plain small corner dots, simplified borders, no colored side strips, and weaker depth.
- The reference card right side has a dedicated vertical divider before the menu column. Candidate cards have no divider. Restore it at about 89% of card width.
- Candidate menu dots are visually close in count/location, but the surrounding right-side construction is flatter and lacks the reference's card-section depth.
- Candidate switches are flatter and the knobs are oversized. The reference uses recessed metallic rounded tracks, fine highlights/shadows, and smaller outlined knobs; match those details for both orange enabled and cream disabled states.
- The reference `Seg–Sex`/`Sáb–Dom` details use dark navy text with an orange separator dot. Candidate detail text is muted blue-gray and the separator is not visibly orange. The second candidate reads `Sáb e Dom`, while the reference reads `Sáb–Dom`; restore the dash and separator styling.
- Reference alarm-card typography has a consistent geometric/digital face and compact vertical rhythm. Candidate title/detail text is heavier/smoother and the time glyphs retain the wrong slashed-zero treatment. Align the title and detail baselines with the reference after restoring the icon column.
- The reference model row contains a circular star/compass icon at the left, left-aligned text, an outlined right arrow, and corner screws. Candidate contains centered text only; restore the icon, left alignment, arrow, screws, and shorter row height.
- The reference plus label uses the same angular display face as the identity. Candidate label is smoother/heavier and horizontally centered, which changes the balance even aside from the missing controls.

**Retro verdict: not identical.** The hero artwork is approximately matched, but the timer card, both alarm cards, model row, plus control, texture, and 3D ornamentation have multiple visible structural differences.

## Nineties

### Highest-priority corrections

1. **Restore the missing countdown display.** The reference next-alarm panel contains a centered silver/gray multi-layer bezel about 70% of panel width, with a dark navy inner screen and large cyan 7-segment `07:30`. The candidate bezel spans roughly 92% of panel width, has a much more panoramic aspect ratio, and its inner screen is blank dark navy. Recreate the bezel margins/aspect ratio and render the cyan digits; this is the largest identity failure.
2. **Bring back the pixel-era typography and placement.** The reference title `PRÓXIMO ALARME`, summary, alarm titles/details, model label, and `+ Alarme` use a chunky pixel/display face. Candidate uses smooth modern sans text throughout. The reference next-alarm title is left aligned near 3–4% of panel width; candidate centers it. Restore the pixel face, weight, and the left alignment, including the compact title/summary sizes.
3. **Restore the missing header alarm clock.** The reference header has a pixel alarm-clock icon at the far left, with the logo starting around 17–18% of canvas width. Candidate has no clock and starts `Desperta` around 7–8% of canvas width. Add the clock in its square/bitmap treatment and shift/size the logo to the reference position and width.
4. **Fix the alarm-card icon/switch/menu system.** Both reference cards have a framed alarm-clock thumbnail, a rectangular teal/gray switch with a square metallic knob, and a small square menu button with a horizontal ellipsis. Candidate has no thumbnail, uses circular knobs on thin tracks, and its menu is oversized with vertical dots. Restore all three controls and their pixel/beveled treatment.
5. **Restore the complete lower controls and spacing.** The candidate model row has no left document icon and centers its label; the reference icon and label are left aligned. The candidate plus bar has no left dark square/yellow plus control and places the label centrally, while the reference uses a layered cobalt bar with an inset left control. Candidate also leaves a much larger gap before the plus bar. Match the compact reference row/bar spacing and controls.

### Other concrete differences

The reference background is comparatively uniform, with only subtle texture. Remove the candidate's repeated stripes and match the darker teal.
- Candidate content panels are visibly inset farther from the sides. Header candidate margins are about 4% of canvas width versus roughly 1–2% in the reference; the same wider teal gutters persist around the large panel, alarm cards, model row, and plus bar. Expand the app-owned panels toward the reference side margins.
- The header frame in the reference has strong white/gray/black multi-line bevels and a dark lower/right shadow. Candidate frame is simpler/flatter. Restore the layered pixel bevel.
- Candidate header blue is more visibly graded from dark left to lighter right, while the reference is a deep navy/blue panel with a subtler treatment. Match the reference blue and its inset frame.
- Candidate gear control is much larger relative to the header (about 13% of header width versus roughly 9–10% in the reference), sits too close to the top/bottom edges, and uses a smooth navy outlined target-like gear. Reference is a smaller silver square with a black blocky cog and pixel bevel. Resize, recolor, and pixelate it.

- The reference has a white horizontal rule below the title with a darker lower edge. Candidate has no rule. Restore it across roughly 90% of the panel's inner width.
- The sunset thumbnail is approximately in the correct centered region and relative size, but candidate framing/bevel is simpler and its vertical relation to the title/display changes because the title/rule/display geometry is wrong.
- Reference countdown bezel margins are roughly 15% of panel width on each side; candidate margins are only about 4–5%. Match the reference inner-screen dark navy, silver outer border, white highlight, and black shadow layers.
- Candidate large panel-to-first-card gap is roughly twice the reference gap. Candidate also has a much larger gap between its two alarm cards (about 6–7% of card width) versus the reference's compact ~2% gap.
- Candidate alarm cards are roughly 7–9% taller after normalization. Their content sits lower within the cards, while the reference content is compact and aligned from the upper-left icon/time column.
- The reference alarm thumbnails occupy about 12–13% of card width near the left edge; candidate thumbnails are completely absent, leaving the digital time at roughly 4% inset rather than the reference's ~19–20% inset.

- Reference model row has a framed blue document/list icon at the left, left-aligned pixel label, and strong beveled frame. Candidate omits the icon and centers smooth text.

**Nineties verdict: not identical.** The candidate preserves only a broad teal/blue/gray arrangement and the sunset thumbnail. Missing countdown digits, missing clock/icon controls, wrong switch/menu geometry, modern typography, changed margins/spacing, and simplified bevels make the identity materially different.
